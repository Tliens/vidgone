Brush over it or draw a box. Slightly cover beyond the logo edges for the cleanest rebuild. Scrub the timeline to check every scene.

Export clean MP4

Every frame is repaired with the same mask, then re-encoded with hardware acceleration. Preview a frame first to tune the method.

Tips for the cleanest result
  • Static watermarks only (v1): the mask applies to the whole video, so the watermark should stay in one place. Moving watermarks are on the roadmap.
  • Quick fix suits plain backgrounds (sky, walls, water); Smart repair handles most logos; Fine repair rebuilds texture — slower but strongest.
  • A watermark over busy motion (faces, text) can’t be perfectly rebuilt by any tool — try Soft blur for an unobtrusive result.
  • Only remove watermarks from videos you own or have permission to edit. Removing others’ credits to repost content may infringe copyright.

FAQ

Is VidGone really free?

Yes — free to use, no signup, and no watermarks added. Everything runs in your browser, so there are no server costs to pass on.

Are my videos uploaded anywhere?

No. VidGone processes video frames locally with WebCodecs inside your browser tab. Your file never leaves your device — you can even disconnect from the internet after loading the page.

Which watermarks can it remove?

Static, fixed-position watermarks: platform logos, channel names, date stamps, text overlays. The cleaner the background behind the watermark, the cleaner the result. Moving / animated watermarks are planned for a future update.

Which browsers are supported?

Any browser with WebCodecs: Chrome and Edge (recommended, hardware-accelerated), Safari 16.4+, Firefox 130+. Export speed depends on your device.

Does exporting reduce quality?

The video is re-encoded as H.264 MP4. Choose High quality to keep more detail; audio is copied losslessly when the source uses AAC.
